01 · number theoryLean statement · review open

Can arithmetic and geometry constrain the same integer?

The Tenth Discriminant. A checked Lean endpoint gives a strict 47-billion bound on the actual discriminant of imaginary quadratic class-number-one fields. Independent semantic review remains open; this is not a Siegel-zero theorem.

What does a twisted connection preserve?

Connection Laplacian. A machine-checked development for finite graphs, including balanced-component kernel dimensions and exact spectral identities. Generalization beyond the formalized hypotheses remains a separate obligation.

Where does a formal reduction stop short of an answer?

Cryptid verifier. Exact Lean criteria for selected Turing machines, bounded comparisons and L17 reachability results. The corresponding non-halting questions have not been solved.

When, if ever, does geometry help a language model?

IGBundle-LLM. Fiber-structured adapter experiments, recorded controls and published corrections. The current evidence does not establish a general performance benefit, learned hyperbolicity or efficiency gain.

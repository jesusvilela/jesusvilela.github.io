## The identity

I build research systems at the boundary of mathematics, software, and
machine intelligence: proof kernels, symbolic tests, provenance ledgers,
geometric architectures, and adversarial review loops.

The public surface is intentionally quiet. Visuals are a map, not a claim;
every technical statement should lead to a public repository, test, paper,
release, or explicit caveat.

## The digital ego, kept honest

This profile is an **interface**, not a simulated person and not an
autonomous authority. Its “ego” is a design metaphor for a stable research
orientation:

- **center** — ask the question before narrating the answer;
- **memory** — preserve lineage, assumptions, and failed routes;
- **doubt** — expose falsifiers and matched nulls;
- **voice** — translate difficult work into public, inspectable artifacts;
- **boundary** — publish deliberate, inspectable artifacts.

## UniverseOS-inspired geometric portrait

The homepage no longer uses the oversized enso/kanji insignia. Instead, its hero includes a deliberately restrained **live 3D WebGL sculpture** adapted as an original browser-side visual from the owner's UniverseOS design vocabulary: nested harmonic shells, seven angled orbital carriers, woven local ribbons, seven points with Fano incidence lines, and a softly pulsing nucleus. This is an **artistic, conceptual projection**—not an embedding theorem, a physiological model, or a claim about machine cognition. No files or private research content from the UniverseOS repository are published or imported.

The \`hero-universe.js\` enhancement uses pinned Three.js \`0.160.1\` from jsDelivr. It loads only when the hero approaches the viewport. Rendering has a capped device-pixel ratio, pauses when the page is hidden or the figure is offscreen, and respects \`prefers-reduced-motion\`. Visitors can drag or use arrow keys to rotate; the small motion control offers pause and resume. If the CDN, WebGL or graphics context fails, an inline SVG remains visible and the research content stays available. The source has no build-step requirement. Actual WebGL display requires a browser with network access to the pinned CDN.

## The curated research map

The [public research projection](https://jesusvilela.github.io/#map-title) is an interactive orientation through a manually selected bibliography of public work from `jesusvilela` and `Dojo-1`. It is a **conceptual visualization**, not a mathematically validated hyperbolic embedding or a directory of every public repository. One selected Hugging Face model accompanies its source investigation, not an independent claim of model performance.

Nothing enters the site simply because it has a GitHub URL or an appealing mathematical title.

## Editorial inclusion standard

Here, **research-grade** is a *site-curation threshold*, not a credential, a journal review, an originality certificate, or a single numerical score. Each included investigation should offer a meaningful research question and inspectable primary material: a precise formal statement and checking path, a documented experiment with controls, or a substantial specification/atlas whose limits are explicit. Readers should be able to identify assumptions, negative results, provenance, and the open remainder.

Formal results, measured experiments, exploratory hypotheses and semantic/editorial structures remain distinct. An AI-generated proof sketch is not proof; a verified Lean encoding warrants confidence in the **encoded statement**, not automatically its intended broader interpretation. Likewise, model releases and passing tests do not prove scientific efficacy.

Authorship and credit must be checked per repository. The programme is human-directed and AI-assisted, with explicit limitations and corrections; presence here does **not** assert sole personal authorship of every contribution or external peer endorsement.

## October 2026 public entry points

The homepage foregrounds six source-linked investigations, with three further language and computation studies in the curated index. The distinction between formal proof, measurement, hypothesis and editorial work remains visible:

- [Tenth Discriminant](https://github.com/Dojo-1/tenth-discriminant): Lean-checked strict actual-field discriminant bound below 47 billion, with independent human semantic review open.
- [Connection Laplacian](https://github.com/jesusvilela/connection_laplacian_lean): checked finite-graph kernel and spectral statements, not blanket assertions about every geometric setting.
- [Cryptid verifier](https://github.com/Dojo-1/cryptid-verifier): exact formal criteria and bounded reachability, not solved non-halting problems.
- [IGBundle-LLM](https://github.com/jesusvilela/IGBundle-LLM): geometric-adapter experiments with corrections, controls and no established general superiority.
- [ESMF-0](https://github.com/Dojo-1/ESMF-0-Mathematical-Genesis): controlled experimental geometric-cognitive substrate, not demonstrated semantic emergence.
- [A Synopsis of Results](https://github.com/Dojo-1/A-Synopsis-of-Results-in-Pure-Applied-and-Computational-Mathematics): five-volume editorial atlas with 5,000 records; the distributed snapshot is not the complete reproducible registry.

The curated index also includes [λSAT](https://github.com/jesusvilela/lambda-sat-solver), [§-LANG S1](https://github.com/jesusvilela/-lang.s1), and [Trasgo §1](https://github.com/jesusvilela/trasgo), each with its own formal, measured or semantic evidence limits.

**Research stance.** The author provides questions, conceptual architecture, prioritization and direction; AI systems assist in derivation, formalization, implementation and verification. These roles are not independent peer review. Claims must be read at the scale of their actual theorem, measurement, hypothesis or semantic object.

**Maintenance.** The site statically features an editorially reviewed set of public repositories. Its client-side code checks GitHub metadata for the two public accounts and updates curated entries only: newly created, unreviewed repositories are never auto-promoted. Private and internal repositories are not indexed. Under temporary API failure, known curated source links remain available and the degraded metadata state is disclosed.

The execution-plane visualization accepts only the explicitly selected [manifoldgl model artifact](https://huggingface.co/jesusvilela/manifoldgl); efficacy or geometry claims must be evaluated against the associated [IGBundle-LLM correction notice](https://github.com/jesusvilela/IGBundle-LLM/blob/main/docs/correction-notice.md). Other Hub models, datasets and demos are not implicitly presented as research contributions. General coding utilities, hackathon prototypes and lifestyle applications are outside this site's research-selection scope even when their repositories remain publicly accessible.
